A Birthday Poem

A Friend Along the Way

We met by chance, just words across a screen,
A simple friendship, unexpected but serene.
Who knew a stranger could become a friend,
Whose laughter makes the hardest days descend?

Perhaps life has its own paths to unfold,
With countless stories waiting to be told.
And as the years pass by and seasons change,
I hope our friendship never feels strange.

We'll grow much older, chasing different dreams,
Through winding roads and life's unpredictable streams.
We may get busy, and our worlds may drift apart,
But I hope you'll always have a place within my heart.

No promises of forever do I need to make,
No expectations, no paths we have to take.
Just genuine friendship, simple and true,
And a little happiness in everything you do.

Someday we'll laugh about the days gone by,
The silly conversations that made the hours fly.
We'll talk about the memories that brought us here,
And smile at how we made it through each year.

So here's a wish beneath the birthday sky,
May happiness find you wherever you fly.
May all your dreams come closer into view,
And may life bring the very best to you.

Happy Birthday, Rae, may your days be bright,
May your darkest moments turn to light.
And as we grow older, whatever life may send,
I hope we still can greet each other as a friend.

Here's to more birthdays, more laughter, and years ahead. May we grow old, live our own stories, and never forget the friendship that started with a simple hello.
